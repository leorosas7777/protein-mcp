## 1. Target profile (UniProt)

<small>Source: [UniProtKB P15056](https://www.uniprot.org/uniprotkb/P15056/entry) · reviewed (Swiss-Prot) · annotation score 5/5 · last annotation update 2026-11-04 · retrieved 2026-11-05T01:17:28Z</small>

### Function

Protein kinase involved in the transduction of mitogenic signals from the cell membrane to the nucleus (Probable). Phosphorylates MAP2K1, and thereby activates the MAP kinase signal transduction pathway (PubMed:21441910, PubMed:29433126). Phosphorylates PFKFB2 (PubMed:36402789). May play a role in the postsynaptic responses of hippocampal neurons (PubMed:1508179)

<small>Evidence: experimental, curator inference · [PMID 1508179](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/1508179/), [PMID 21441910](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/21441910/), [PMID 29433126](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/29433126/), [PMID 36402789](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36402789/)</small>

## 7. Methods and limitations

This document is generated deterministically from the dossier files listed at the top. It contains no interpretation: values are quoted from the source records, every link is built from an identifier returned by the source, and fields without data are shown as 'no record'. Selections are partial by design; each section states how many records were shown out of how many were found.

Selection rules and caveats stated by the tools that produced the data:

- PDB structures are a diverse selection, not the complete list.
- AlphaFold is a single static prediction without ligands, nucleotides or ions.
- All links are built from IDs/URLs returned by RCSB and AlphaFold DB.
- Ligands are listed clinical-stage first (max_phase), then by best pChEMBL: potency is not selectivity or developability, and many top values are single measurements (see n_measurements).
- ChEMBL max_phase: 4 approved, 3/2/1 clinical phase, 0.5 early phase 1, -1 clinical but phase unknown to ChEMBL, None preclinical.
- Compounds with a recorded mechanism on this target but no qualifying IC50/Ki/Kd (typically antibodies and other proteins) are listed in mechanism_compounds_without_qualifying_activity with name, type and phase: they are drugs too.
- Potency is grouped by (mutation, standard_type, assay_class); cellular and biochemical values and different mutations are never pooled. 'not stated' does NOT prove wild type.
- Activities whose curated mutation label disagrees with the mutation named in the assay text are excluded and listed in label_conflicts_flagged.
- Only compounds with a recorded mechanism or inside the top-N are checked for max_phase: other clinical-stage compounds may exist among the ligands not returned.
- Only exact '=' relations enter the statistics; censored values (>, <) are counted in activities.excluded.
- name is ChEMBL's preferred name and is None for most research compounds; no names are inferred.
- Linking PDB ligands to ChEMBL (by InChIKey) is not done here: no match means 'no record', never a guess.
- 1 compounds with a recorded mechanism have qualifying data only for other variants than 'V600E' (mechanism_compounds_other_variants_only): they are not untested; they have no qualifying measurement on this variant in ChEMBL (one may exist but be excluded: censored relation, low confidence, label conflict).
- Truncated: the 6000 most potent of 9683 activities were fetched (lowest pChEMBL fetched 7.23; activities tied at that value may be partly missing), plus every activity of the 14 compounds with a recorded mechanism. ligands_with_qualifying_data is therefore a lower bound. For other compounds, n_measurements, median and worst values only count measurements above that cutoff, and weaker ligands are not seen. With a mutation filter, activities of that variant below the cutoff are also missed.
- Search matches the gene symbol or protein name in title/abstract; ambiguous symbols can bring unrelated papers, so check titles.
- Only abstracts are returned, not full text.
- All links are built from IDs returned by Europe PMC.

Structure preparation rules (prep/manifest.json, decisions):

- Original files are kept unmodified in raw/. Loop completion, protonation and charges are Phase 3.
- Receptor (clean/<PDB>_receptor.pdb): target chains only (RCSB mapping), first model, first alternate conformation, no hydrogens, no waters, no additives, no ligands; nucleotides, cofactors and metal ions MG/MN/ZN/FE/CO/NI/CU that touch a target chain are KEPT (e.g. KRAS GDP + Mg, 8TVK); NA, K, CL, CA, CD are removed. Coordinates are unchanged. PDB format unless chain names or atom count do not fit, then mmCIF.
- Pocket: target-chain residues with any atom within 5 Å of the ligand instance (gemmi, first alternate conformation of the ligand), in the asymmetric unit only: crystal-symmetry copies are excluded (they put residues ~47 Å away in 8C7Y). UniProt numbers come from label_seq_id + RCSB alignment; residues of other polymer chains are listed apart. No pocket for nucleotides and cofactors (kept in the receptor), for ligands with fewer than 10 heavy atoms, or without a target-chain neighbour in RCSB: each is listed in pocket_not_computed. Box: ligand bounding box + 5 Å per side, same frame as the mmCIF; sides above 30 Å are flagged. A ligand instance with pocket residues in more than one target chain is flagged spans_target_chains (e.g. a bivalent compound bridging two sites): its pocket and box cover every chain it touches and are NOT split into one box per site; residues_by_chain gives the count per chain.
- Chain metrics: missing_uniprot_ranges are residues of the sample sequence not modelled inside the modelled span; a missing range next to a pocket residue is flagged. variant_check reads the residue actually modelled at the variant position in each target chain and compares it with the UniProt canonical letter (not the letter typed in the filter): the variant can be named in the title and still be unmodelled in the chain that holds the ligand (3OG7 A). Two residues at one position (microheterogeneity) give status 'ambiguous'.
- AlphaFold pLDDT (B-factor column, equal to _ma_qa_metric_local) is read at the same UniProt numbers as each experimental pocket, only for the canonical-sequence model; residues without a UniProt number are not compared (alphafold_residues_compared vs n_residues).
- Target chains come from RCSB's chain-to-UniProt mapping, not from the mmCIF _struct_ref, which is unreliable (3OG7 cites UniProt Q5IBP5; antibody chains often cite no UniProt).
- Chains are given as label_asym_id (mmCIF) and auth_asym_id (author, used by PyMOL and the PDB format); they can differ (7ZR0: BRAF is label D, auth K).
- Ligand poses are the crystal coordinates of each instance (RCSB ModelServer, by label_asym_id). A ligand can sit on one chain only (3OG7: chain A) or touch several.
- neighbor_*_chains come from RCSB rcsb_target_neighbors with the closest distance per chain; RCSB does not document the distance cutoff.
- construct.mutations are the depositor's declared mutations of the target entity; they can include solubility/engineering mutations besides the variant (3OG7 declares 13). Any declared mutation means the structure is not the native sequence; an empty list does NOT prove it is native (the variant may be named only in the title, or the text may be unparsed, e.g. 'YES').
- Additives (ions, buffers, cryoprotectants) get no SDF. The classification is the one of get_structures; some additives (e.g. BEN, EOH in 5JSM) are not in its list and count as ligands.
- Glycans (RCSB branched entities, e.g. NAG chains) are not queried and are absent from nonpolymer_instances.
